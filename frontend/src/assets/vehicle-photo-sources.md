# Vehicle photo sources

The catalogue uses these matching model photographs when a vehicle has no custom photo URL:

- Toyota Yaris (2020–2024): [Wikimedia Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2020-2024_Toyota_Yaris.jpg), photo by TTTNIS, CC0 1.0.
- Renault Clio (2023 generation): [Wikimedia Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2024_Renault_Clio_1.6_E-Tech_Hybrid_espirit_Alpine_in_Iron_Blue_at_Renault_Manchester_03.jpg), photo by MoCars, CC0 1.0.
- Peugeot 3008 (2024): [Wikimedia Commons file](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:2024_Peugeot_3008.jpg), photo by Oleg Yunakov, CC BY-SA 4.0.

The images are loaded from Wikimedia Commons. An internet connection is needed to display them; the catalogue falls back to its vehicle illustration if an image cannot be reached. Vehicle-specific image URLs entered in the form are saved with the vehicle and take precedence over these suggestions.
